Had a second session running a tabletop role playing game as game master today, using the daggerheart system in the world of [Genshin Impact](https://siowyisheng.github.io/blog/on-the-merits-and-problems-of-gacha-games/), with quite some homebrew. It was really fun seeing where the dice and our imaginations took us. 

The platform, [Owlbear Rodeo](https://www.owlbear.rodeo/), worked well this time for me and made it easy to run, especially with the GM's Daggerheart extension.
