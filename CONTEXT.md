# Life, Design, Games, Code

A personal blog by Siow Yi Sheng. Posts are written and published from a phone or desktop browser.

## Language

**Post**:
A single written piece with a title, a date, a body and one or more Tags.
_Avoid_: article, entry

**Tag**:
A label attached to a Post, used to filter and search. Replaces the old single "category" field; a Post may have several.
_Avoid_: category

**Core tag**:
One of the four Tags that name the blog: `life`, `design`, `games`, `code`. They have a place in the Masthead; other Tags do not.

**Draft**:
A Post that is saved but not visible to readers.
_Avoid_: unpublished, WIP

**Publish**:
Make a Post visible to readers.

**Masthead**:
The blog title at the top of every page, made of five links: the logo (shows all Posts) and one link per Core tag (shows Posts with that Tag).
